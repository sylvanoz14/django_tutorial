ZONING & APPROVALS NOTE
  Wyndham City Council requires a Town Planning permit for any
  "Place of Assembly" (land where people congregate for entertainment,
  meetings, or cultural activities) — a play centre falls under this
  definition regardless of underlying zone. Budget time/cost for this
  approval step; industrial-zoned sites may need an additional permit
  for change of use from warehouse/logistics to indoor recreation.
